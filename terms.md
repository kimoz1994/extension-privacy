## What the service does

YouTube Summarizer generates a TL;DR and timestamped key takeaways for the
YouTube videos **you choose**, using an AI model. Summaries are generated
automatically and can be wrong — treat them as a fast first read, not a
guarantee. The service is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by
YouTube or Google.

## Free and Pro tiers

- **Free**: 3 summaries per day, on videos up to 30 minutes long. No payment
  method required.
- **Pro — $4.99/month**: raises you to 12 summaries per day and unlocks longer
  videos. Purchases are processed by Paddle as our merchant of record; the
  subscription renews monthly until you cancel. The limits in effect are always
  stated plainly in the extension and on this site's pricing page.

There are no accounts: the extension uses an anonymous install ID. Keep your
Paddle receipt email — it is how you restore Pro after reinstalling.

## Canceling

Cancel any time from the link in your Paddle receipt email (or Paddle's
customer portal). You keep Pro until the end of the paid period; after that the
install returns to the Free tier. See the [Refund policy](refunds.html).
