## 14-day, no-questions-asked

If you paid for Pro and want your money back, email **jenoughdev@gmail.com**
with the email address from your Paddle receipt **within 14 days of the
charge** and we will refund that charge in full.

## After 14 days

Email us anyway — we handle these case by case. If something was broken on our
side (the extension didn't work for you), we refund it. Partial months are
refunded at our discretion.

## Canceling anytime

You can cancel whenever you like from the link in your Paddle receipt email.
Canceling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you
already paid for. Canceling and refunding are separate — canceling alone does
not refund the current period.

## How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued through Paddle (our merchant of record) back to your
original payment method. After Paddle processes them, banks typically take
5–10 business days to show the money. After a refund, this install returns to
the Free tier.
